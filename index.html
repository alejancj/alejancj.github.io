    About me

    My curiosity about how the world works drives me to keep learning, and it is what led me to study engineering. I enjoy designing practical solutions and pushing myself to grow as an engineer.

    I am drawn to complex, high-stakes technical problems, and I want to join teams that solve them. I want my work to help expand what we know and what we can build.

    Throughout my studies I have taken on projects and team roles that pushed me outside my comfort zone. My experiences gave me hands-on industry exposure and let me apply what I learned in class. Along the way I have built skills in design, written and verbal communication, and working across disciplines.
